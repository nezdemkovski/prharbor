Settings concepts

Everything the current app lets you change, minus the row toggles the new list no longer needs, plus what the timeline adds: freshness thresholds, bots, stacks, snooze and a couple of new notifications. All controls work; nothing is saved.

A. One page, inside the popover

Replaces the list like the current app does, with a back button. Everything on one scrolling page, grouped, with a search field that filters the rows as you type.

B. Sidebar, like System Settings

Sections on the left, one at a time on the right. Better if settings live in their own window rather than the popover.
